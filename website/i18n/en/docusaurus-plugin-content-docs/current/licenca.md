## Why AGPL

Because Confere is a network service, the AGPL ensures that anyone who hosts it with modifications also shares the source of those modifications with its users. Using, studying, modifying and redistributing it is free.

## Third-party data

The data comes from [BrasilAPI](https://brasilapi.com.br/) and the public sources it aggregates (Receita Federal, Banco Central, IBGE, CVM and others). It is not covered by this license and follows each source's own terms.
